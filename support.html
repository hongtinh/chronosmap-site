FAQ

  • ChronosMap: open a sample map from the library, or open a .chronosmap file from your device.
  • ChronosMap: enter edit mode to add people, events and trajectory points, or import them from photos.
  • yunji & ChronosMap: when you import photos, the app reads their time and location to build trajectory points.
  • Your data stays on your device (and your own iCloud for yunji if enabled). We don't upload it to our servers.
  • ChronosMap runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac; yunji on iPhone and iPad.
